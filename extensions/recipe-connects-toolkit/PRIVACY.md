# Privacy — Recipe Connects Toolkit

- No account and no sign-in.
- We do not collect, store, or sell personal data.
- **Copy clean link** runs on the current tab in your browser only; the cleaned URL is copied to your clipboard locally.
- **Print-friendly view** opens a local extension page and may request the current page URL to show readable content. No data is sent to Oceanink servers.
- The only network request is from your browser directly to the recipe site you already opened (for print view). We do not operate a backend.
