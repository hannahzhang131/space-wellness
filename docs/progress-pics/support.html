Progress Pics support

Progress Pics is a simple weekly progress photo app (front, side, back) for iPhone. There is no sign-in and no server. Photos, weight and notes stay on your device.

Contact

Email recipeconnects@oceanink.co. We usually reply within a few business days.

Lifetime purchase and restore

Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through the App Store (progresspics.lifetime). It is not a subscription.

  • New phone or reinstall? Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that bought Lifetime, open Progress Pics, then tap Restore purchase on the paywall or in Settings.
  • Restore does not bring back photos. It only restores the paid features. To move photos, use an iPhone backup or export images from the app.
  • Wrong Apple ID? Purchases are tied to the Apple ID used at purchase. Another Apple ID will not show Lifetime until that account bought it.

Free version

You can save up to six weekly check-ins without paying. Compare, reminders and optional Face ID lock still work on the free tier.

Permissions

Camera and photo library access are only for your check-in photos. Face ID and notifications are optional and controlled in Settings.

Not medical advice

Progress Pics is a personal photo record. It does not provide medical, diet or fitness advice.
