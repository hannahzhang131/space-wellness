RentProof support

RentProof helps you photograph rooms at move-in and move-out and export a PDF report. There is no sign-in and no server. Photos and reports stay on your device.

Contact

Email recipeconnects@oceanink.co. We usually reply within a few business days.

Lifetime purchase and restore

Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through the App Store. It is not a subscription.

  • New phone or reinstall? Use the same Apple ID in the App Store, open RentProof, then tap Restore purchase in Settings or on the paywall.
  • Restore does not move your photos. It only unlocks paid features. Use a device backup to move data.

Free version

One home, standard rooms, limited photos per room, and PDF with a preview mark.
