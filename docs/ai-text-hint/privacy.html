AI Text Hint privacy

No collection

We do not collect, store, or sell personal data. There is no sign-in and no third-party analytics in the extension.

On-device analysis

When you click Analyze, the extension reads text from your active browser tab or your selection and scores patterns on your device. Text is not sent to Oceanink servers or external AI services.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting are used only when you use the extension on the tab you already opened. There is no broad background access to every website.

Not a detector guarantee

Scores are informational hints about writing patterns, not proof of human or AI authorship.
