Pattern & Slop Checker support

Analyze selection works best on forums (Reddit, etc.): highlight one post or comment, then open the extension.

Google Docs: select text first, then open the popup and tap Analyze selection (v0.2.2+ uses your last selection). Pro highlight is not supported in Docs.

Low scores are common and do not mean “human” — the tool looks for template-like patterns only.
