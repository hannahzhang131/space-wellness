Progress Pics privacy notice

Who we are

Progress Pics is published by Oceanink Ltd, 124 City Road, London, United Kingdom, EC1V 2NX. Company number 16684370.

Photos, weight and notes

Your progress photos, weight and notes stay inside the app on your phone. Progress Pics does not upload them, does not copy them to your photo library, and has no account. If you share an image, it is then handled by the app you chose.

Camera and Face ID

The camera is used only to take your progress photos. If you turn on the lock, iOS checks Face ID or your passcode. The app never sees your face data.

Reminders

The weekly reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server sends it.

Purchases

Lifetime is a one-time purchase through Apple, not a subscription. Apple processes the payment. Oceanink does not receive your card number. The app asks Apple whether your Apple ID already bought Lifetime. There is no separate account to sign into.

What we do not do

Progress Pics has no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across other companies’ apps.

Not medical advice

Progress Pics is a personal photo record. It does not give medical, diet or fitness advice.

Children

Progress Pics is not directed at children under 13.
