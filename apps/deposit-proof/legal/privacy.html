Deposit Proof privacy

Who we are

Deposit Proof is published by Oceanink Ltd, 124 City Road, London, United Kingdom, EC1V 2NX. Company number 16684370.

Photos and reports

Room photos, notes, and the PDF report stay on your device. Deposit Proof does not upload them and does not have an account. If you share or print a PDF, that file is then handled by the app you chose.

Location

When you add a photo, the app may ask for location so it can write a street address under that photo. If you say no, the report uses the home address you typed. Location is not tracked in the background and is not sent to Oceanink.

Purchases

Lifetime is a one-time purchase through Apple, not a subscription. Apple processes the payment. Oceanink does not receive your card number. The app asks Apple whether this Apple ID already bought Lifetime. There is no separate account to sign into.

What we do not do

Deposit Proof has no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across other companies’ apps.

Children

Deposit Proof is a household record. It is not directed at children under 13.

Not legal advice

A report can help you remember the condition of a home. It is not a legal opinion, and it does not guarantee a deposit outcome.
